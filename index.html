📖 How To Use Tether

Tether is a mindful consumption tracker. It is designed to help you pace yourself, reflect on your usage, and prevent automatic or excessive habits.

1. The Core Loop & Intentions

Complete your Pre-Flight Routine to unlock your daily session. You must select a Focus Task before starting to ensure you have an objective. When finished, hit End Session & Log to recap your feelings and task accountability.

2. Methods (Vape, Joint, Edible)

Tether adapts to your method. Vapes use timed breathing cycles. Joints log hits without the breathing circle. Edibles bypass hits entirely and ask for a direct milligram dosage, adjusting your pharmacokinetic decay curve to account for onset delay.

3. Ween-Off Protocol & T-Breaks

If you configure a Yearly T-Break month in Settings, Tether will automatically activate "Ween-Off Mode" exactly one month prior. This mode slowly halves your session limits, reduces draw times, and increases mandatory wait times to step your tolerance down.

4. Locks, Breaks & "Fuck It" Passes

When you reach your limits, the dashboard locks you out. Quick Hits provide a tiny microdose bypass, but trigger a short T-Break penalty. "Fuck It" passes entirely bypass the lock for a full session, but reset your monthly automatic T-break tracker and flag your calendar.
