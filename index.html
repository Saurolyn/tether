📖 How To Use Tether

Tether is a mindful consumption tracker. It is designed to help you pace yourself, reflect on your usage, and prevent automatic or excessive habits.

1. The Core Loop & Intentions

Complete the checklist for your chosen Session or Mini Session. Each pool resets Monday. Used tokens begin cooling when the session starts, even if discarded or the first draw is cancelled. Fuck It tokens start extra full Sessions and reset on the first of each month. They bypass breaks and cooldowns, but never Hardcore Lockout or the Session checklist.

2. Methods (Vape, Joint, Edible)

Tether adapts to your method. Vapes use timed breathing cycles. Joints log generic hits and duration. Edibles ask for straight milligram dosage and bypass breathing visuals entirely.

Cart THC vaporized is estimated using draw seconds × 1.2–2.2 mg oil per second × THC fraction. The displayed average is the midpoint of this illustrative range, not a measured value or a prediction of absorbed THC. Flower hits have no timed estimate, and edible label amounts are shown separately. Hits with a recorded THC percentage retain it; older hits use their linked profile's current percentage, so editing that profile can change past estimates. Methodless hits linked to a cart profile are treated as cart hits. Missing or invalid data cannot produce an estimate.

3. Tokens & Calendar

Configure tokens, per-session waits, hit limits, cooldowns and strict breaks in Settings. An optional Fuck It penalty begins when the session starts. Calendar day plans remain reminders, not restrictions. Future availability assumes no new sessions or settings changes. Existing history is preserved.
