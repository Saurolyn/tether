📖 How To Use Tether

Tether is a mindful consumption tracker. It is designed to help you pace yourself, reflect on your usage, and prevent automatic or excessive habits.

1. The Core Loop & Intentions

Complete your Pre-Flight Routine to unlock your daily session. You must select a Focus Task before starting to ensure you have a goal while elevated. When finished, hit End Session & Log to recap your feelings and task completion.

2. Methods (Vape, Joint, Edible)

Tether adapts to your method. Vapes use timed breathing cycles. Joints log generic hits and duration. Edibles ask for straight milligram dosage and bypass breathing visuals entirely.

3. Ween-Off Protocol & T-Breaks

If you configure a Yearly T-Break month in Settings, Tether will automatically activate "Ween-Off Mode" exactly one month prior. This mode slowly halves your limits and increases wait times to prepare your tolerance.
